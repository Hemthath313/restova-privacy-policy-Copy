## 1. Introduction

RESTOVA: Restaurant Billing (“RESTOVA,” “the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a restaurant billing and point-of-sale application designed to help users manage restaurant operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use RESTOVA.

## 2. Information stored by the App

The current version of RESTOVA stores its application data locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, locally stored data may include restaurant details, menu items, prices, inventory records, bills, orders, sales records, table information, and other business information supported by the App.

If you enter personal information about customers, employees, or other individuals, that information may also be stored locally as part of the relevant restaurant records.

**Important:** The exact categories of information stored depend on the App's implemented features and the fields you enter. This policy should be checked against the current release before publication.

## 3. Cloud storage and data transmission

The current version of RESTOVA is designed to store application data locally. It does not currently use Supabase as a cloud backend.

When the App's features operate entirely locally, the restaurant records stored by those features are not uploaded to a RESTOVA cloud backend.

Third-party SDKs, operating-system services, or other network-enabled functionality must be reviewed separately before making broader claims that no information is transmitted outside the device.

## 4. How information is used

Locally stored information is used to provide the App's available restaurant-management functionality, such as billing, order management, menu management, inventory management, table management, and reports, where those features are implemented.

Information may be accessed or modified through the App's relevant features.
